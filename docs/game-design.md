# Game design

Where the game is going: an open town in the shape of GTA, played as a dog. Roam freely, take a car, cause chaos, later do missions. The chaos is a spaniel's: affection and enthusiasm, never harm.

What exists now is the dog and a small town to try him in.

## The dog

A golden cocker spaniel: long wavy ears, red collar, docked wagging tail, tongue out.

| He does | How |
|---|---|
| Trot | Steer. He turns on the spot and stops when you let go. |
| Zoomies | Keep running flat out for about a second (or run at birds) and he tips into a gallop: half as fast again, ears flying, dust. He has no brakes and skids wide round corners. |
| Jump | Works standing, trotting and galloping. In the air he can still lean, enough to hop onto a bench or the table from a standstill. |
| Bonk | Gallop straight into a tree, lamp, bench or the hedge: stars, a dazed moment, leaves fall. A glancing hit only slides him past. |
| Say hello | Trot into a person: paws up on their chest, hearts. Fly into them (jumping or galloping): they sit down with a bump. |
| Break stuff | Run into pots, bins, cones, boxes and a ball. Pots smash, bins spill. Jump on the picnic table and the plates go flying. Each thing counts once on the paw counter. |
| Birds | He notices any flock nearby: his head turns, he yips, a bird appears in a thought bubble. Standing still he freezes and points. Run at them and he goes straight into zoomies; they fly off to another spot and the bird counter goes up. Barking scares them too. |
| Bark | Startles people and birds near him. |
| Play bow | Left alone for a few seconds, he asks for a game. |
| Drive | Near the car a wheel bubble and a yellow button appear. E (or jump onto the car) and he hops into the seat, paws on the wheel. |

## The car

An open red car, one in town. Steer where it should go; steering against the way it faces brakes and then reverses. It is quick (about one and a half times a gallop), slides a little, knocks over anything loose, scares birds, and people hop out of its way. Hitting something solid stops it with a crunch and a bounce. Bark is the horn. E hops out over whichever side has room.

## The town

A park (trees, benches, a picnic table, flower pots, a sandy cross of paths with a plaza) inside a ring road with pavements and zebra crossings, houses beyond, a hedge round the edge. Three people (two walk, one stands), three flocks of birds.

## Rules that will hold

See *Design rules* in `CLAUDE.md`: not violent, he is a dog, the camera never turns, one finger is enough.

## Not decided yet

- Missions, and what the counters are for.
- Camera: fixed and looking north, as in wheek. A GTA-style camera behind the car would break "up means away"; to decide when driving matters more.
- How big the town gets, more cars, traffic, people who react for longer (chasing him, calling him).
