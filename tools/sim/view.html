A springer spaniel's bones and joints in a physics engine (MuJoCo). The motion is not drawn by hand: her joints are driven within a dog's joint ranges and strength, starting from a rough guess, and a search finds the leap that reaches the asked height with the least effort and lands on its paws. For the trot and the gallop each joint follows a repeating curve, and the search finds the curves that keep the asked speed, stride after stride, without falling. Dark legs are the near side, pale the far side. Dots under a paw mean it is on the ground.
