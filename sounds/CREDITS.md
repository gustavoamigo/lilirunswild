# Sounds

`lili-bark.wav`, `lili-grumble-1.wav` and `lili-grumble-2.wav` are Lili herself, recorded by Gustavo Amigo
on 2026-11-13 and cleaned of background noise. They are covered by `../LICENSE` like the rest of the repository.
They are the only recorded sounds in the game; everything else is synthesised in `src/audio.js`.
