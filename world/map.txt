# A ring road with a middle road and two cross streets, and six blocks inside it: houses round lawns, the park (middle,
# top), the playground (east, top), the town centre (middle, below the park: shops round a square with a fountain and
# market stalls), and the lake (west, below: a sandy beach all round it). Houses line the ring outside; Lili's home and the school are among them on the north
# side, facing the road (the camera looks north, so buildings north of a road show their fronts).
#
# East of the town, through a gate in the houses opposite the middle road, the big park: woods all round, a path north
# to south through them, a wide meadow in the middle, a round pond with ducks (north), and the ice cream van at the end of
# the main path from the gate.
#
# South of it all, the beach: sand the whole way along, two paths down to it between the houses (and the park's path
# through the woods), sandcastles, umbrellas, seagulls; then the sea, as far as can be seen (no horizon: the camera
# looks north). Lili can swim out a little way.
